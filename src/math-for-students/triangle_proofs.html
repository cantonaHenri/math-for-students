How to Write a Geometric Proof

A geometric proof is built one logical step at a time: each line is either something you were given, or something that follows from a rule you already know. Work through the examples below one line at a time to see how a full proof comes together.

The Problem

Given: Segments AB and CD intersect at point E.
AE = DE, CE = BE
Prove: (a) ΔAEC ≅ ΔDEB
(b) AC = DB
Given: Triangle ABC is isosceles with AB = AC. D is the midpoint of side BC.
Prove: (a) ΔABD ≅ ΔACD
(b) AD ⊥ BC
