Linear Functions: y = mx + b

A "linear function" is just a math rule that draws a straight line. m tells you how steep the line is (the slope), and b tells you where the line crosses the y-axis (the y-intercept).

y = mx + b

Section 1: Examples

💡 Tip: To make the table, pick some x values (like -2, -1, 0, 1, 2) and plug each one into the formula to get y. For example, if y = 2x - 5, then when x = 3: y = 2(3) - 5 = 1.
